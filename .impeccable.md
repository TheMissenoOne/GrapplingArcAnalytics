# Design context — GrapplingArcAnalytics internal tools

> Derived from the repo, the project's stated ethos, and the brief for the event registrar.
> NOT collected in a teach interview — if any of it is wrong, it is my inference, not your word.

## Users

One operator: the maintainer of this corpus, working alone, at a desk, in long sessions
(this repo's commit clock runs past midnight regularly). The job is to watch a video frame
and record what it shows — hundreds of times per sitting. Everything is local; nothing here
is ever seen by a customer.

The scarce resource is the operator's attention, and the expensive failure is a wrong event
entering the corpus, because a wrong label propagates into an athlete's graph and their
rating and nothing downstream can tell it from an observed one.

## Brand personality

**Forensic, unhurried, honest.**

The whole project is built on one distinction: what was observed versus what was inferred.
`points` is absent rather than zero when unread. A technique nobody saw stays generic. A
rejected event is kept and marked, not deleted. The interface has to carry that same posture
— it should feel like a lab bench or a broadcast logging station, an instrument that records
what you tell it and never flatters the record.

## Aesthetic direction

**An instrument, not a dashboard.** Reference points: a Steenbeck flatbed editing table, a
broadcast logging sheet, a lab notebook. Anti-references: admin-panel card grids, KPI tiles,
anything that decorates.

**Dark**, derived not defaulted: the content is video stills in full colour, and a dark
surround stops the chrome competing with the only thing being judged. Same reason a colour
grading suite is dark.

**Type**: Bitstream Charter for everything readable — Matthew Carter's 1987 face, drawn to
stay legible when the rendering is poor, which is exactly the job of a caption beside a
grainy 720p still. JetBrains Mono only where digits must align in columns (timecodes,
scores) — functional, never as a costume for "technical". Both already installed on this
machine, so the tool never depends on a network to look right.

**Colour**: near-black slate, neutrals tinted to the same cool hue, one signal amber for the
playhead. Accents are rare on purpose. The mats in this footage are blue and yellow, so any
UI colour that could be mistaken for mat is disqualified.

## Design principles

1. **The frame is the interface.** Chrome recedes; the still and the filmstrip get the room.
2. **One focus, always.** The search never loses focus. Every other control is a key, not a
   click, because a hand that leaves the keyboard costs a second per event.
3. **Identity is displayed, never assumed.** Both competitors and their keys stay on screen
   permanently — mis-attributing an actor is this domain's most expensive error.
4. **Absent beats zero.** The interface must make "I could not tell" as easy to express as
   any value, and must never fill a field to look complete.
5. **No save anxiety.** Work persists as it happens; the state is shown, not requested.
